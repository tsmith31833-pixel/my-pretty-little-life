Challenge of the Month

The Holiday Calm Fund

Build a small cushion for holiday spending so January starts calm. One step each week, with an email to guide you.

0 of 4 weeks done
Week 1Make your list

Write down every gift, event, and trip you expect this season.

Week 2Set your number

Add it up and choose a realistic amount to set aside.

Week 3Find the money

Pause one subscription or skip two takeout meals, and move the difference.

Week 4Spend with a plan

Shop from your list and track every purchase. No January regret.

Weekly Money Date

Prompts for your Sunday money date.

Pick one prompt each week or draw one at random. Members get a fresh prompt by email every Sunday.

This week’s prompt What is one money win from this week, big or small?
Sunday Reset checklist
Check in
  • What did I spend this week that I feel good about?
  • Which purchase surprised me?
  • Which bill is coming up next?
Reflect
  • How did money make me feel this week, in one word?
  • Where did I spend on autopilot?
  • What would I do differently next week?
Plan
  • What expense can I plan for now?
  • How much will I move to savings this week?
  • Which day this week will be a no-spend day?
Celebrate
  • What is one money win, big or small?
  • Which habit am I proud of keeping?
  • How will I treat myself within my budget?

Our mission

Financial confidence should feel good.

Money & Grace Co. exists for women who are balancing careers, families, responsibilities, and big personal goals, and who are tired of money advice that feels cold, complicated, or judgmental.

We create simple tools and approachable education that help you budget better, save more, and pay down debt, without giving up the things that make life feel lovely.

Luxury lifestyle, meet practical money management.

What we believe

Our values

Grace

Over guilt

Every money mistake is information, not a character flaw. We start where you are.

Simple

Over complicated

A plan you can follow beats a perfect plan you abandon by the 12th.

Pretty

And practical

When your tools are beautiful, you actually want to use them. That is the whole point.

Honest

Always

Clear education, no hype, no get-rich-quick promises. Just habits that add up.
